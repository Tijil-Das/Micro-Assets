Obstacle Avoidance Car • Navigation Algorithm
▶ Robot Loop Running
Trigger Ultrasonic Pulse
digitalWrite(trigPin, HIGH) • 10 µs
Measure Echo Pulse Duration
duration = pulseIn(echoPin, HIGH)
Calculate Distance
distance = duration / 58.2 (in cm)
Distance Check
Evaluate Range
distance > 15 cm
0 < distance < 10 cm
🟢 Absence of Obstacle (Forward)
• Center Servo: Myservo.write(90)
• Drive DC Motors Forward:
MRb = HIGH, MRa = LOW // Right
MLb = HIGH, MLa = LOW // Left
🔴 Presence of Obstacle (Avoidance)
1. Stop Motors: All pins LOW (100 ms)
2. Sweep Servo: 0° → 180° → 90° (500 ms)
3. Reverse: MRa=HIGH, MLa=HIGH (500 ms)
4. Pause: All pins LOW (100 ms)
5. Steer Left: MRb=HIGH (500 ms)
