How to use

Add: drag a part onto the board.

Move: drag its body.

Wire: click a terminal (dot), then click another terminal or empty space to drop a branch node, then continue clicking to route/split the wire. Click a terminal again to finish. Press Esc to cancel a wire.

Delete: select a component / wire / junction, press Delete.

Rotate: select a component, press R.
